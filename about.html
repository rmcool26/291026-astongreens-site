History

How we got here.

Rooftop beginnings

We began as a small rooftop solar contractor and helped shape India’s solar PV industry in its early growth phase.

A strategic shift after COVID

We redefined our business model, expanded abroad and quickly took a leading position in on-ground EPC.

Efficiency through technology

1.3 GWp delivered on time

Skills training institutes

Our in-house training institutes, run under our CSR initiatives, build the skilled workforce behind our projects.

06 · Scale2023

Terranxt incubated at IIT Delhi–FITT
