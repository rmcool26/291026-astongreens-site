# Home

- File: `index.html`
- Audience: GCC tender evaluators, India C&I buyers, investors, partners
- Purpose: In 10 seconds say what Astongreens is, how big, and how to contact.
- Primary CTA: Contact our team (text links: Request a proposal; CEO appointment in the closer)
- SEO title: Astongreens: solar EPC for the Middle East and India

## Sections (in order)
1. **Hero** (dark, full screen). Scroll-driven sun path, glow, whispers, 4-fact strip. Photo or drone loop placeholder.
2. **Statement + numbers** (paper). Word-by-word scroll reveal sentence, then count-up numbers (5+ GW, ~50 MW, 4 countries, 10+ yrs). Footnote: USD 25M target, company profile PDF.
3. **What we do** (white). Sticky scrollytelling: left panel = step counter, progress bar, visual; right = 5 services, each linking to its page.
4. **Where we work + projects** (sand). Country tabs + map placeholder, 3 project rows.
5. **Technovision** (dark). 4 rows: BIM, SmartTrak AI, Terranxt, Odoo ERP.
6. **Clients and partners** (paper). 6 logo placeholders.
7. **ESG and training** (white). Two text blocks with links.
8. **News** (sand). 3 rows + link to Life at Astongreens.
9. **Closing CTA** (dark).

Culture, sports and festival posts do NOT appear on Home (they live on life.html).
