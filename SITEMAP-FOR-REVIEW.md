## What to check on each page
1. Content: is anything wrong or missing? Facts, names, numbers. Text in [square brackets] is still to confirm.
2. Design: spacing, alignment, anything that looks broken or dull.
3. Links and buttons: do they go to the right place?
4. Phone and tablet: open at 390 px and 768 px wide. Does anything overlap or get cut?
5. Scroll: does anything jump, lag or feel stuck? (Especially About > History.)
6. Feedback format: Page name, section name, what is wrong, what you expect, screenshot.
